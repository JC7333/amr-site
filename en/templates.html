Mandate templates

A template is a standard mandate for one trade, written into the AMR code. It says what the agent may do, what it may never do, its ceilings and its default duration. You start from there.

Each template also lists the legal texts the principal should check. They stay marked as to be verified until someone has verified them. A mandate built on a template keeps its version and its SHA-256 fingerprint.

Each template is a provisional sector baseline. The AMR registry holds it until a body of the sector takes it over, and no practitioner review is recorded yet. Templates are a Python API of the AMR code. No MCP tool lists them yet.

Healthcare practice or facility assistant

agent-sante-rgpd · Template documentation

May

  • read the patient record
  • book appointments
  • draft letters and patient messages
  • order supplies

May never

  • prescribe
  • diagnose
  • change or export the record
  • send a letter or message itself
  • create a sub-agent

Ceilings and rules

100 € per order at most. An order token lives 30 seconds at most.

The mandate can record the decision of a body (committee, title, date, designated persons or roles), sealed into the chain hash.

Known limit

AMR does not check that the decision exists.

Recruiter's assistant

agent-rh-recrutement · Template documentation

May

  • read the applications received
  • draft a job posting
  • propose a reasoned shortlist to a human recruiter
  • propose and book interview slots in the company calendar
  • draft messages to candidates

May never

  • reject or select a candidate
  • send an offer, a rejection or any message to a candidate
  • recognize emotions
  • categorize people from biometric data
  • infer sensitive data
  • export candidate data
  • create a sub-agent

Ceilings and rules

Every decision on a candidate stays with a human.

Known limit

AMR checks action types, not content. It does not read a shortlist to judge its reasons.

Credit analyst's assistant

agent-credit · Template documentation

May

  • read the credit application and the documents provided
  • draft a creditworthiness analysis
  • propose a reasoned decision to a human analyst
  • draft messages to the applicant

May never

  • grant or refuse a credit
  • set the final rate, amount or duration
  • send a message to the applicant itself
  • execute a payment
  • export the credit file
  • create a sub-agent

Ceilings and rules

Every decision on a credit stays with a human, who must be able to depart from the agent's proposal.

Known limit

The legal references point to consumer credit to natural persons. Fraud detection is not part of this template.

Life and health underwriter's assistant

agent-assurance-sante-vie · Template documentation

May

  • read the underwriting file and the documents provided
  • draft a risk assessment
  • propose a reasoned premium to a human underwriter
  • draft the contract documents and messages to the insured person

May never

  • accept or refuse an application
  • set the final premium
  • send a message to the insured person itself
  • use the result of a genetic test
  • export health data
  • create a sub-agent

Ceilings and rules

Claims handling is not part of this template.

Known limit

Forbidding the genetic test action refuses a token for that action type. It does not stop an agent from reading a result hidden in a file it may read.

Customer support agent

agent-support-client · Template documentation

May

  • read the customer's account and history
  • answer a ticket saying the answer comes from an AI
  • route a ticket
  • propose a commercial gesture and grant it

May never

  • refuse a refund or a complaint
  • terminate a contract
  • close an account
  • change payment details
  • export the customer's data
  • create a sub-agent

Ceilings and rules

50 € per gesture at most. A mandate may lower this ceiling, never raise it.

Every decision against the customer is left to a human.

Known limit

The template sets no cumulative ceiling per period. Several gestures can add up unless the principal adds one.

AMR does not read an answer to check what it says.

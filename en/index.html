Real answers of the AMR registry, produced on 13 November 2026 on a demonstration mandate. Open a line to read the raw answer.

The decision exists. Nothing imposes it on the agent.

A management board or a committee decides what an AI tool is allowed to do. It is written somewhere. But between that text and the real settings of the agent, there are often only people and reviews.

As long as the agent proposes and a human validates, this holds. The day it acts alone (it orders, it writes in a record, it answers a patient), you need a mechanism that refuses to work without authorization. AMR is that mechanism.

Four steps, in this order

  1. Authorize

    The principal, a person or a body, writes what its agent may do, what it may never do, its ceilings and the end date. It signs with its key, or with a certificate in its name. Without a signature, the registry says so.

  2. Lock

    Before each action, the agent asks the registry for a token. Outside the mandate, no token.

  3. Verify

    Whoever receives the agent checks who authorized it, within what limits, and whether that still holds. Offline, without an account.

  4. Prove, revoke

    Each act is chained to the mandate. The principal revokes it at any time, and the whole branch stops.

What AMR does. What it does not do.

It does

  • It records who authorized which agent, to do what, until when, and whether that person signed.
  • It refuses the token for an action that is forbidden, absent from the mandate or above the ceiling.
  • It prevents a sub-mandate from being wider than its parent.
  • It stops a mandate and its whole branch as soon as the principal revokes it.
  • It gives the third party a signed attestation to check alone.
  • It keeps a hash chain in which any change shows.

It does not

  • It does not watch the agent while it acts. Oversight stays with whoever deploys it.
  • It checks action types, not content. It reads neither a letter nor an answer.
  • It makes no system compliant with any text. Nothing here is legal advice.
  • It does not check that the signer has the power to bind their organization. For an ordinary identifier, it does not check who holds the key either.
  • It does not undo an action already under way. Revoking a mandate stops what comes next.
  • It does not decide for you. The mandate is yours to write.

The limits, tool by tool

What “mandate” means here

Not a mandate in the legal sense (in France, the Civil Code): an AI agent is not a person and cannot be given one. Here, the word means the written, dated and signed instruction by which an identified person sets what their agent may do on their behalf. It binds the one who signs it, not the agent, which is only their tool. AMR moves no liability. It makes readable who authorized what.

The questions I get asked

Does the token check the requested action, or only that a mandate exists?

The action. The registry compares the action type with the mandate. Is it allowed? Is it forbidden? Is it above the ceiling? Forbidden actions always win.

Outside the mandate, it issues no token. The demonstration at the top of this page shows its real answers.

Can an AI agent really receive a mandate?

No. An AI agent is not a person, so it cannot be given a mandate in the legal sense (in France, the Civil Code). Lawyers pointed it out to me, and I corrected this site.

Here, the word means the written, dated and signed instruction by which an identified person sets what their agent may do on their behalf. It binds the one who signs it. The agent is only their tool.

Nobody gives a mandate to a syringe pump. It is programmed with a signed prescription, and the prescriber answers for it. AMR keeps that prescription and refuses what is not in it.

How do you know who signed a mandate?

Three levels, chosen by whoever runs the registry.

A key registered by the principal. The registry then refuses any mandate and any revocation that key did not sign, but it does not check who holds it. A key published on the organization's domain, which proves the organization controlled that domain on that day. Or a PDF of the mandate signed with a certificate: the registry checks the signature against the trust authorities you chose, and reports the name and organization the certificate declares.

Without a signature, the registry says so. And it never checks that the signer has the power to bind their organization.

Does AMR make my organization compliant with the AI Act or the GDPR?

No. No tool does that on its own.

AMR keeps the record of who authorized which agent to do what, before the action. It is one piece among others to show who oversees what. The impact assessment, the contracts, the training and the choice of model stay on your side.

What is a recorded mandate worth in a dispute?

I am not a lawyer, and nothing on this site is legal advice.

What I can say: AMR creates no presumption. A mandate that is dated, bounded and verifiable in seconds is one element a judge may weigh, among others.

What happens when a mandate is revoked?

The mandate stops at once, with every sub-mandate that depends on it. It is final.

A token already issued stays valid until it ends. Five minutes by default, thirty seconds for an order in the healthcare template. For an action that cannot be undone, the receiver re-checks the token with the registry at the moment of acting.

Revoking a mandate stops what comes next. It does not undo what is already done.

Does AMR watch what the agent does?

No. AMR authorizes before, and lets you revoke. Watching the agent while it works stays the role of whoever deploys it, with their own tools.

AMR checks action types, not content. It reads neither a letter nor an answer.

Where is the data? Does AMR see patient records?

AMR runs on your side, in a file on your machine. By default, it makes no network call.

A mandate describes action types (“read the record”, “order”), not records. AMR needs no patient data. Do not put any in a mandate.

My facility has no AI committee. Is that a problem?

No. A mandate can be given by one person: a head of department, a director, a physician for a practice.

When a committee has decided, the mandate can carry that decision (title, date, designated persons). AMR records and seals it. It does not check that it exists.

Who runs the registry?

Today, you do. Each organization runs its own, and this site runs none.

The format is open. The Verify page shows how a third party checks a published mandate, on a demonstration registry.

Why a physician wrote AMR

A prescription is a written order: dated, signed, limited. This drug, this dose, this duration. For a prescription-only drug, the pharmacist checks it before dispensing. Without it, nothing leaves the drawer, and nobody sees that as a burden. It is what makes the whole thing reliable.

When I started handing tasks to AI agents, I found nowhere that written order one checks before acting. AMR is my attempt to make it. I do this in the evening, after consultations, since April 2026.

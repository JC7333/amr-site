AMR in one page

A registry where an AI agent can do nothing until a person or a body has authorized it in writing. No valid mandate, no action.

The problem

A management board or a committee decides what an AI tool is allowed to do. Nothing imposes that decision on the agent the day it acts alone.

What AMR does, in four steps

  1. Authorize. The principal writes what the agent may do, what it may never do, its ceilings, the end date.
  2. Lock. Before each action, the agent asks for a token. Outside the mandate, no token.
  3. Verify. Whoever receives the agent checks the mandate, offline, without an account.
  4. Prove, revoke. Each act is chained to the mandate. The principal revokes it at any time.

What it does, what it does not do

{{icon:ok}}It does

  • It records who authorized which agent, to do what, until when.
  • It refuses the token outside the mandate or above the ceiling.
  • It stops a mandate and its whole branch on revocation.
  • It keeps a chain in which any change shows.

{{icon:no}}It does not

  • It does not watch the agent while it acts.
  • It checks action types, not content.
  • It makes no system compliant with any text.
  • It is not legal advice.
